A predictive sensorimotor module gives a vision-language-action policy explicit physical context for contact-rich manipulation.

SPA-VLA augments a π0.5-style policy with a six-layer multimodal Transformer trained to anticipate future force/torque, joint effort, and terminal joint-state displacement. Its hierarchical features are injected throughout the action expert to support precise interaction under contact.

Method

The SPA-VLA Model

SPA receives multiview visual tokens, force/torque history, robot state, and joint-effort history. Predictive pretraining turns these signals into shallow, middle, and deep physical representations that condition six levels of the action expert.

01

Predictive pretraining

A multimodal Transformer processes visual tokens together with force/torque, robot-state, and joint-effort histories. A shared decoder reads every retained layer and predicts future force/torque, future joint effort, and terminal joint-state displacement, shaping a hierarchy that captures contact response, actuation load, and motion intent.

02

Depth-aligned action augmentation

During action learning, selected Action Expert layers query the corresponding SPA representations through gated cross-attention. Independently learned gates regulate each residual update, injecting progressively integrated physical context while preserving the pretrained action pathway.

Quantitative evaluation

Success across all six tasks.

Success rates are measured over 20 consecutive trials per task. Tasks follow the hard-to-easy order in the experimental setup; Overall reports total successes over 120 trials.

Primary result 90.0% 108 / 120 successful trials

Predictive sensorimotor augmentation improves performance across difficulty levels.

Under the same backbone and evaluation protocol, SPA-VLA reaches 108/120 successful trials, compared with 74/120 for π0.5, 83/120 for ForceVLA-π0.5, and 87/120 for ForceVLA2-π0.5. These correspond to gains of 28.3, 20.8, and 17.5 percentage points, respectively. SPA-VLA achieves the highest success rate on five of the six tasks.

The gains remain consistent from hard tasks (87.5% mean) to medium (85.0%) and easy tasks (97.5%). Three-Hole Plug Insertion is the only task not led by SPA-VLA, where ForceVLA2-π0.5 reaches 85% compared with 80% for SPA-VLA.

Matched qualitative comparison

Maintaining the insertion axis under contact.

Both policies begin the Three-Hole Plug task with closely matched plug-to-socket alignment. As contact loads build, π0.5 develops lateral drift and ends off-axis. SPA-VLA preserves axial alignment and directs the applied force vertically downward, completing the insertion.
